٢. الجهاز الدوري:
* Definition:
Coagulative necrosis of tissues due to physical
or chemical agents.
* Etiology:
1. Physical:
A. Thermal
1. Direct: flame
2. Indirect: scald
B. Electrical
C. Radiation
2. Chemical:
A. Alkalis
B. Acids
N.B. cold burn
* Classification of burn:
A. According to extent
Minor:<10 %
Intermediate: 10 - 30%
Major: >30%
B. According to depth
First degree: epidermis only
Second degree: epidermis part of dermis
Third degree: all skin
* How does burn harm the body?
1- Increased Capillary permeability
2- Evaporation of water → severe
dehydration
* Complications:
A. Systemic:
1. Respiratory:
Asphyxia, laryngospasm, bronchospasm, type
2 Respiratory failure
2. C.V.S.
Shock hypovolemic
